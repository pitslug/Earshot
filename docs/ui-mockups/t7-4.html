Decided (built in above)

  • Exact hardware names. Each row says what Earshot runs on that part right now, and follows the model and "where it runs" choices.
  • A model that is too slow here is marked and stays selectable. It is hidden only when it cannot fit in memory at all.
  • The check runs automatically after a download. When it says too slow, the user is told and may keep the model.
  • Four transcription choices. Onboarding asks which languages are spoken: any European language recommends Parakeet v3 (also when English is ticked), any other language Whisper large-v3-turbo.
  • Whisper large-v3-turbo says plainly that it is less accurate and slower than Parakeet. The page says nothing about its speed on a given computer; the benchmark (T7.4c) shows it.
  • No summary timings on the page: the wait depends on the meeting length and on summarising during the meeting. Timings belong to a later benchmark (T7.4c).
  • Forcing summaries onto the graphics card with a model too big for it runs that model on the processor, with a note.
  • No larger summary models: the 27B and 30B-A3B measured no better than the 9B.
  • Models not chosen can be removed, showing the space freed. Helper models are never listed. Settings keeps the left section list.
