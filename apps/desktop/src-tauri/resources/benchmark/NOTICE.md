# Benchmark meeting

The two tracks in this folder are the benchmark meeting of Settings > Benchmark (T7.4c), made by `cargo xtask bench-meeting` from the AMI Meeting Corpus.

- Source: AMI Meeting Corpus, meeting ES2004a, from 570 s to 720 s (individual headset recordings `ES2004a.Headset-0.wav` to `ES2004a.Headset-3.wav`), https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/
- Authors: J. Carletta et al., "The AMI Meeting Corpus: A Pre-announcement", MLMI 2005; University of Edinburgh and the AMI Consortium.
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (corpus licence: https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/license.shtml).
- Changes: cut to the span above; `mic.flac` is the Project Manager's headset, `reference.flac` the sum of the other three headsets; both tracks levelled to the same loudness while speech is active and stored as 16 kHz 16-bit mono FLAC.
